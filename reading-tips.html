Small steps, good stories

Build a reading habit that feels good.

You do not need to read for hours every day. Start small, choose books that interest you, and make the experience your own.

01

Start with ten minutes

Attach reading to a routine you already have, such as after breakfast or before bed. A short session still counts.

02

Keep a book nearby

Leave your current book somewhere convenient so it is easy to pick up during a break.

03

Follow your curiosity

Try fiction, history, science, poetry, or comics. There is no single correct way to be a reader.

“A little reading can make an ordinary day feel different.”
