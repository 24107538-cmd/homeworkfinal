The reading list

A shelf for every mood.

Here are a few reading moods to inspire your next library visit or bookstore browse. These are visual prompts rather than a ranked list.

Quiet afternoons

Choose a thoughtful story and give yourself time to get lost in it.

Fresh perspectives

Try an essay or memoir that introduces you to a different point of view.

One more chapter

Pick a page-turner that makes you curious about what happens next.

Learn something

Explore a topic you have always wanted to understand better.

Slow and cozy

Pair a gentle read with a warm drink and a quiet moment.

Library wandering

Browse the shelves without a plan and see what catches your eye.

A simple rule

You do not have to finish every book you start. Reading should be rewarding, so give yourself permission to choose something that fits you.
